## Long description

**The machines took the mines. You're going in to take them back.**

Ascent is a six-degrees-of-freedom shooter. There's no up or down, just the tunnel ahead. Pilot the WR-9 gunship through ten hostile mining complexes, fight the rogue robots that took them over, find the access keys, destroy the reactor and race to the exit before the countdown hits zero.

### Features
- **10 mines.** Each has its own layout and character: tall lava shafts, narrow twisting drill tunnels, wide caverns, glass galleries and a final core that mixes everything. Branching tunnels, loops, dead ends and hidden rooms reward exploring.
- **Full 6DOF flight.** Strafe, roll and climb in any direction. Fight upside down. Use the automap when you're lost.
- **19 robot types and 3 bosses.** Swarmers, snipers, mine-layers, drillers, cloakers and shielded heavies, each with its own behaviour.
- **12 weapons.** 6 primaries, from pulse cannons to a rail lance and a singularity gun, plus 6 missile types: homing, cluster, swarm, buster, EMP and mines. Weapons grow stronger as you upgrade your lasers.
- **Escape sequences.** Destroy the reactor, then fly for your life before the mine collapses.
- **Rescue survivors and find secrets.** Each mine hides stranded miners and secret rooms behind breakable panels.
- **Voiced story.** 11 narrated story scenes follow the campaign from first shift to the core.
- **4 difficulties,** from Recruit to Nightmare, with a rank for every run.
- **Keyboard and mouse, or gamepad.**

### Made from code
Every ship, robot, mine, texture, sound effect and song in Ascent was generated from code. There are no store-bought or downloaded assets apart from two open-licence fonts.

### Controls (defaults)
- **Mouse:** aim. **W / S:** forward and back. **A / D:** strafe. **Space / Ctrl:** up and down. **Q / E:** roll. **Shift:** afterburner.
- **Left click:** primary weapon. **Right click:** missile. **G:** next missile. **1–6:** pick weapon. **F:** flare. **R:** rear view. **Tab:** automap. **Esc:** pause.
- Full gamepad support. Every key and button can be rebound in Settings → Controls.

### Builds
- **Play in browser:** works best in Chrome or Edge. Use *Toggle Fullscreen* in the pause menu.
- **Linux:** one self-contained executable. Download, mark as executable (`chmod +x Ascent.x86_64`) and run. Needs a GPU with Vulkan drivers.
